## Make responsibility visible

Identify who defines the operating requirements, supplies controls, installs connections and verifies the agreed sequence. A responsibility matrix helps the owner, consultant and trades see the interfaces. It should be agreed for the particular project rather than assumed from trade titles.

## Review information before installation

Discuss the submittals and existing records required for coordination. Record outstanding questions and assign someone to resolve them. Approved equipment information and the project sequence should be coordinated through the responsible team; an article cannot supply a sequence suitable for a specific plant.

## Schedule the handoffs

Controls work depends on the readiness of other parts of the installation. Ask how the programme identifies installation, connections, verification and any follow-up visits. Include access and the availability of people needed to witness or discuss the checks.

## Record the final arrangement

Ask what controls documentation and operating information will be included in turnover, and how unresolved items will be tracked. Glenayre provides a related boiler upgrade scope, with completion to be confirmed. This general coordination guide does not reproduce or approve that project’s operating sequence.

## Planning checklist

- Agreed responsibility matrix
- Submittal and question register
- Verification activities in the programme
- Owner representative availability
- Turnover and outstanding-item records
