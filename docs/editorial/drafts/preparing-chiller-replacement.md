## Survey the route as well as the equipment

A replacement enquiry should describe the equipment location, available access information and the surrounding occupied areas. Existing drawings and photographs can support early discussions. The project team must assess actual site conditions; do not assume a new unit can follow the original installation route.

## Define specialist coordination

Ask who is responsible for access planning, lifting arrangements, supports, electrical readiness and connected services. These activities need project-specific planning and appropriate specialists. Detailed lifting instructions and sensitive site access information do not belong in a public case study.

## Put readiness into the programme

Discuss what must be complete before delivery, installation and startup. Identify constraints around building use and proposed interruption windows. A programme should show dependencies and the person responsible for confirming readiness, rather than treating delivery as the finish date.

## Plan the handover

Ask how startup, testing and balancing where required, documentation and outstanding work will be coordinated. Collingwood is a related chiller replacement scope, with final completion to be confirmed. It illustrates the type of work to discuss, not a reusable lifting plan or a promised duration.

## Planning checklist

- Equipment location and existing information
- Access review responsibility
- Supporting trade interfaces
- Occupancy and interruption constraints
- Startup and turnover requirements
