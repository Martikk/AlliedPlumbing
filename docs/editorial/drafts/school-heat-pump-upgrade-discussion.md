## Begin with the school brief

Define the rooms and building needs under consideration before selecting equipment. Share the existing system information, known comfort concerns and available drawings. A school upgrade needs a project-specific assessment; a generic equipment description cannot establish the appropriate scope.

## Bring the supporting disciplines together

Ask who will review electrical requirements, equipment placement, supporting construction and controls. Discuss noise considerations, maintenance access and the relationship with retained systems. These are coordination questions for the project team, not a substitute for design or a checklist of approvals already obtained.

## Plan around school use

Provide the school calendar, proposed work windows and the spaces that could be released. Identify the school contact who can confirm access and changes. Discuss how deliveries and disruptive activities will be coordinated before assuming that the whole project can fit within a holiday period.

## Keep claims tied to evidence

Ask which performance expectations will be assessed and how project requirements will be verified. Do not assume an energy saving or completion date from the equipment type alone. The Herbert Spencer ASHP scope is a related reference; its final completion and closeout remain to be confirmed.

## Planning checklist

- School requirements and existing records
- Electrical and controls review responsibilities
- Placement and maintenance access
- School calendar and contact
- Project-specific verification requirements
