## Define what the project requires

Testing and balancing requirements should be established for the actual scope by the responsible project team. Owners can ask which systems and areas are included and what records are expected. This article explains coordination questions; it does not set technical acceptance values or replace a specialist’s procedures.

## Allow time for readiness

Discuss what installation and controls work must be ready before the relevant checks can take place. Make these dependencies visible in the programme. Booking an activity without confirming readiness can leave unresolved work and additional coordination at the end of a project.

## Agree how findings are handled

Ask who receives reports, reviews findings and coordinates corrective work if needed. Define how outstanding items are recorded and when follow-up checks will be discussed. A report is useful when the team understands what action it requires and who owns that action.

## Include records in turnover

Identify the project records expected in the handover package and who will explain them to the building team. Leigh included coordination with testing and balancing agents. That documented scope does not establish the requirements for every future school or building project.

## Planning checklist

- Project-specific requirements
- Readiness dependencies
- Responsible specialist and coordinator
- Finding and follow-up process
- Records for owner turnover
